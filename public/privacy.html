Bai-Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Bai-Tracker is a field data collection tool published by Bai Engineering. It is used by our staff and by our clients' project teams to record survey and inspection points, and to attach photos, videos, and notes to them. It is not a consumer app: accounts are created by Bai Engineering on request, there is no public sign-up, and each account can reach only the projects it has been assigned.

This policy covers the Bai-Tracker iOS app and the Bai-Tracker web app.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Your email address and password To create and authenticate your account. Authentication is handled by Amazon Cognito; we never receive or store your password in readable form.
Point records: latitude and longitude, elevation, location name, category, description, comments, and the date, time, and time zone of creation This is the project data the app exists to record.
Photos, videos, and audio recordings you capture or attach To document the point you are recording.
Your device's precise location, while the app is in use To show where you are on the map and to fill in the coordinates of a new point. Location is only read while the app is open, and only when you have granted permission.

All of it is linked to your account, because project records need to be attributable to the person who filed them.

What we do not collect

  • We do not track you, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, calendars, health data, or browsing history.
  • Photos are resized before upload, which strips the camera metadata embedded in the original file, including any GPS coordinates the camera recorded. The point's own coordinates are stored separately, as described above.

Where your information is stored, and who can see it

Project data is stored in Amazon Web Services in the US West (N. California) region: point records in Amazon DynamoDB, media files in Amazon S3, and accounts in Amazon Cognito. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest by AWS.

Access is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the app. Each project is assigned to a group, and a signed-in user can read or change records only for the projects their account belongs to. Bai Engineering administrators can access all projects in order to operate and support the service.

Third parties

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the service and stores your data on our behalf, under our instructions.
  • Google Maps Platform provides the map and place search. Displaying the map and searching for a place sends the necessary request data to Google — for place search, that includes the text you type and the area of the map you are searching near. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple TestFlight, while the app is in beta testing, provides Apple with installation and crash information about test builds under Apple's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Project records and media are kept for as long as the project engagement is active, and afterwards for as long as we need them as a record of our work. Accounts are disabled when a person no longer needs access.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the location, camera, microphone, and photo library permissions in iOS Settings at any time. The app still works; the features that need them do not.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information associated with your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. Where the data belongs to a client project, we will coordinate with that client before deleting project records.

Children

Bai-Tracker is a professional tool. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be communicated to account holders directly.
